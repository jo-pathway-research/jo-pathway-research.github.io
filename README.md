# Jo's Pathway Research Lab LTD

**Independent research laboratory based in Ukraine**

---

### About

Jo's Pathway Research Lab LTD specializes in the study of molecular and cellular mechanisms underlying skeletal muscle growth and adaptation.

Our primary research focus is on key signaling pathways, including:

- mTORC1/2  
- Akt/PKB  
- Androgen receptor signaling  
- IGF-1/PI3K  
- Myostatin–Smad pathway  
- TGF-β  
- BMP signaling  
- Wnt/β-catenin  
- FOXO  
- FGFR  

We investigate how various pharmacological agents and biologically active compounds influence these pathways, modulating gene transcription, protein synthesis, satellite cell activation, myofibrillar hypertrophy, and body composition.

---

### Research Focus

- Molecular mechanisms of muscle growth and adaptation  
- Pharmacological modulation of signaling pathways  
- Inter-individual variability and genetic factors  
- Pharmacokinetics and long-term physiological effects  
- Endocrine, cardiovascular, and metabolic outcomes  

---

### Mission

To collect, critically analyze, and systematize scientific data in order to build an accurate, evidence-based understanding of the mechanisms of human growth and adaptation.
